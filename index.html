The LG specifies Ocean Black Protection — a proprietary coating applied to the copper coil and fins that guards against corrosion from salt air, industrial pollutants, smoke, and humidity.

What is ISEER and why does it matter more than the star rating?

ISEER — Indian Seasonal Energy Efficiency Ratio — is the Bureau of Energy Efficiency's standardised measure of how much cooling an AC delivers per unit of electricity consumed across a full Indian seasonal usage cycle. Star ratings are derived from ISEER bands, but two ACs with the same star rating can have meaningfully different ISEER values within the same band. In this comparison, both ACs are 3-star rated, but the LG's ISEER of 4.43 versus the Voltas's 3.81 means the LG delivers 16% more cooling per unit of electricity. Over a full year of Indian AC use, that difference amounts to 205 fewer units consumed by the LG — roughly ₹1,640 at standard residential tariffs. When comparing ACs within the same star category, always look at the ISEER value printed on the BEE label, not just the star count.
